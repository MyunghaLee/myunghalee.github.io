Diffusion Model Inference Optimization with CUDA

Course project

Implemented CUDA kernels to accelerate a U-Net within a diffusion model, achieving a 6.6x speedup (16.7s to 2.5s) and the fastest inference time among 40 students.

CPU Design & Hardware Optimization

Course project

Designed and simulated a 5-stage pipelined CPU from scratch in Verilog, compliant with the TSC ISA, including an advanced branch predictor and a 64B unified write-back cache.
