Design Guidelines for High-Throughput Packet Processing Applications on SmartNIC Datapath Accelerators

Undergraduate thesis

A study of how packet processing applications should be structured to achieve high throughput on SmartNIC datapath accelerators. I observed a 28.9% performance improvement in a NVIDIA sample application by applying the guidelines.

KV Cache Replication for Latency-Critical LLM Requests

Research project

A research project on accelerating latency-critical LLM requests by replicating key-value cache within the network, aiming to reduce response times for interactive AI workloads. I was responsible for design, implementation, and evaluation as a third author. The paper is currently under review.

PRC: Process-centric Rate Control for Transient Stragglers in Hybrid-Parallel Training

Research project

A study on mitigating transient stragglers in hybrid-parallel distributed training at the transport layer. I contributed to its rebuttal and revision process and am acknowledged in the paper. The paper is accepted at ACM CoNEXT 2026.
